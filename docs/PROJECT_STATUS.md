## Known limitations
1. One physical store per server; ownership does not transfer when the owner leaves.
2. Membership is server-session only. Helpers must request access again in a new server.
3. Employees have bounded task automation and fixed per-shift wages, but do not simulate schedules, demand forecasts, morale or full shelf maintenance.
4. Mobile layout, multi-server lease failure paths, malicious-client stress, development funnel ingestion and Studio behavior for Phases 3/4 remain unverified.
5. Production universe ID, publishing and commerce are not configured. Development funnel ingestion and Creator Dashboard reporting remain unverified.
6. Product variety is limited to four SKUs and one pending shipment total, with no supplier selection or purchasable commercial plots. Four existing shelves and the checkout lane/queue can be rearranged, and the rear retail floor can be expanded, but arbitrary department construction and additional product departments remain unimplemented. Customer archetypes use reproducible weighted preferences and a persisted satisfaction score, but there is no loyalty, substitution, patience or demand effect. The first storefront palette is fixed and cannot be edited. The Chaos Director has four reproducible schedules for spill/outage, with no wider live event pool yet.
