# Visual Design Bible

## Identity
Modern, colorful, welcoming, polished, expressive, slightly stylized realism. Create a distinctive original neighborhood business with soft architectural edges, readable silhouettes, rich but controlled color, warm light and gameplay-first sightlines. Avoid generic tycoon blocks, unauthorized trademarks, noisy clutter, excessive bloom and visually unrelated asset packs.

## Palette and semantic roles
| Role | Direction | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Brand/deep structure | Deep teal | Fascia, headings, structural accents |
| Warm base | Warm cream / warm white | Walls, UI surfaces, broad interior surfaces |
| Positive/action | Fresh green | Success, fresh produce, confirmed actions; pair with icon/text |
| Attention/chaos | Soft coral | Event warning and urgent prompts; never sole signal |
| Premium/highlight | Muted gold | Rewards and restrained trim |
| Supporting | Natural wood, soft gray, light blue | Fixtures, neutral information, refrigeration |

Choose consistent Roblox material properties during the first Studio art pass and record actual values before treating them as a locked token set. Keep text/background contrast high; do not rely on hue alone.

## Exterior composition
Compact storefront with a legible entrance, broad clear glazing, designed fascia/sign, canopy and trim, warm interior visible from outside, accessible sidewalk, two restrained planters, cart return, one small parking/delivery edge, roofline variation, streetlights and trees. Frame the entry as the arrival focal point. Build expansion as attached wings/raised bays using the same façade rhythm and material system.

## Interior composition
Entrance sightline points to the checkout and clear next objective. Organize one main aisle, a few varied shelf bays, refrigerated wall, produce display, back storage access and employee zone. Preserve broad circulation for carts and NPCs. Use distinct department markers, warm ceiling fixtures and a limited number of promotional endcaps. Fictional packaging uses simple color-block labels and original names.

## Materials and asset conventions
Painted plaster, tile/polished concrete, brushed metal, natural wood, frosted/clear glass, plastic fixtures, fabric accents. Reuse a small material palette; bevel/round only where silhouette benefits. Reuse meshes and textures; avoid unnecessary high-resolution surfaces and excessive unique parts. Register external assets with source, license, ID, modifications and performance notes.

## Lighting strategy
Bright, warm, readable interiors; balanced entry exposure; soft shadow direction outside; controlled highlights; modest warm accents at checkout and signage. Test a clear daytime baseline before optional evening presentation. Avoid heavy post-processing and reflections. Actual Lighting/Atmosphere values, Roblox technology compatibility and mobile cost must be measured in Studio before approval.

## Neighborhood and characters
Begin with one compact commercial block: local road, sidewalk, trees, streetlights, a restrained adjacent storefront/plot and delivery access. Keep distant buildings as low-detail context. Stylized customer variety comes from silhouette/outfit/accessory sets and a small number of expressive reactions. Animation and particle effects should be brief and readable.

## UI and presentation
Use deep teal for primary actions, warm cream surfaces, green success, coral warnings and muted gold rewards. Consistent typography hierarchy, rounded but not oversized cards, clear icon plus text, touch-sized controls. Effects never obscure the objective and can be reduced. Final numeric tokens, font choices, lighting values and device screenshots remain Phase 1 art decisions pending Studio review.

## Visual gate
Review exterior and interior in normal gameplay camera and screenshots at desktop/mobile framing. Pass only when entry, checkout, route, next action, store scale and material cohesion are readable; no placeholder geometry is presented as release art.
