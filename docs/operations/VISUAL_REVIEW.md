# Supermarket visual review checklist

Use the private development experience and the Rojo setup steps in `QUALITY.md`. Do not mark this pass as approved until you have viewed it in Roblox Studio.

## Exterior and arrival

- [ ] From the road/sidewalk approach, the fascia clearly reads **SUPERMARKET CHAOS** and the glass entry is easy to find.
- [ ] The colored canopy frames the door; its support posts, stripes, promise sign and window framing do not block the walking route or obscure the interior.
- [ ] The cart-return shelter and parked carts read as a supermarket feature and do not overlap trees, the runaway-cart path, or the entrance.
- [ ] The storefront's warm interior, aisle silhouettes, produce and checkout are visible through the glass.

## Interior and circulation

- [ ] On entering, the player can distinguish grocery aisles, fresh produce, cold drinks and checkout at a glance.
- [ ] Aisle signs face the entry and do not collide with the ceiling beams, lights, products, or player camera.
- [ ] Shelf products and stocked Citrus Pop cans are visible, aligned with the shelves, and do not float or block the stock prompt.
- [ ] Produce crates/fruit and refrigerator bottles remain visible through the glass case; the cold-drinks sign is readable.
- [ ] The checkout conveyor, scanner, POS screen, bagging surface and overhead sign read as one register. The customer and cashier do not block the interaction prompt or the walk-up lane.
- [ ] The customer, cashier and stock clerk silhouettes look like people at normal gameplay distance, and hidden figures stay invisible until their gameplay state requires them.
- [ ] Walk from the door to the shelf, produce and checkout without hitting the new fixtures; verify ProximityPrompts remain reachable.

## Framing and performance

- [ ] Capture one exterior approach screenshot and one interior screenshot from the entrance aimed toward checkout. Record device, camera position and any readability issue.
- [ ] Check the same interior route in Studio's phone and tablet device emulation; the world and HUD must leave movement and interaction areas clear.
- [ ] Look for washed-out light, reversed text, overlapping geometry, z-fighting, floating parts and visual clutter. Capture exact Output errors if any.
- [ ] Compare normal gameplay camera at player height, not only an overhead editor view. Keep visual approval pending until these observations are recorded.

## Known scope

This is a procedural blockout pass using original built-in Roblox parts. It improves supermarket recognition and fixture variety but does not supply final art meshes, detailed textures, animated NPCs, audio, or a measured performance result.
